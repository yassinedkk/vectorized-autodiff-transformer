# Dataset notice

`shakespeare.txt` is the Tiny Shakespeare corpus used by the character-level transformer experiment.

Source used by the project:
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/karpathy/char-rnn/master/data/tinyshakespeare/input.txt

The corpus contains works attributed to William Shakespeare and is included here solely as the project dataset. The MIT license in this folder applies to the software and course-derived material; it does not replace any terms that may apply independently to the dataset.
